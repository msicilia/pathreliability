Reliability report: TextureBlind

./sample_data · 6 classes · 480 training and 120 test patches · no patient or slide ids · embedding dimension 6

This report measures how a linear classifier on the frozen embeddings of this model behaves beyond its accuracy. Each value is shown beside the range obtained for 11 pathology encoders in the method paper. The ranges give context; they are not thresholds, and the report states no verdict.

  • The patches carry no patient or slide ids, so the split and the tuning of the classifier are at patch level. If several patches come from the same patient or slide, accuracy and calibration are likely to be optimistic.
  • Tuning check was not assessed: the patches carry no patient or slide ids.

The gate

A patch is rejected when its Mahalanobis distance exceeds the novelty threshold (tau_ood = 12.68); otherwise the classifier abstains when its confidence is below the confidence threshold (tau_conf = 0.964). Both were set on 96 validation patches taken from the training split and applied unchanged to everything below.

Method

Linear classifier: standardised embeddings, multinomial logistic regression, C selected among 7 values from 1e-06 to 1 by 3-fold cross-validation (folds stratified by class, log-loss). Split: test side: one of 5 folds, stratified by class, at patch level (no patient or slide ids). Classifier metrics: 5 splits of the pooled embeddings; detection, blur and gate: the split stored in the embeddings file. Calibration error: 15 equal-width bins. kNN: k = 50. The model was not retrained. Input: 224 pixels, mean 0, 0, 0, standard deviation 1, 1, 1. Reference ranges: minimum, median and maximum over the encoders and the tasks of one kind, of the value of one encoder on one task; from “A Retraining-Free Reliability Audit and Abstention Gate for Frozen Pathology Foundation Models”. Tool version 0.2.0, report schema 2.0.
